# Daily Social Posts Guide

- This feature drafts a fresh Threads thread and an Instagram post (card-news carousel or narrated reel) every day for admin approval. Every daily post promotes One Cup Daily (the KakaoTalk morning expression, first day free); tracking links land on `/daily`. Do not steer daily posts back to the offline meetup without a product decision. It is separate from marketing templates (`app/lib/features/growth`), which repeat one post on a schedule.
- Lifecycle (table `social_daily_posts`, one row per channel per KST day): the Vercel cron `/api/cron/social-drafts` (20:07 KST) drafts tomorrow's posts → an admin edits/regenerates/reschedules/approves in `/admin/threads` and `/admin/instagram` (`components/DailyPostsPanel.tsx`, via `/api/admin/social`) → the `marketing` Edge Function tick publishes approved posts when due, records a `marketing_cron_runs` row (`trigger = 'daily'`) and a `growth_posts` row, and marks drafts nobody approved by their time as `expired`. Editing an approved post sends it back to draft.
- Content (`server/content.ts`): a weekday pillar sets the kind of post (insight, expression, compare, scene, quiz, news, weekly; Threads Sun→Sat: insight, expression, quiz, compare, insight, scene, weekly; Instagram: insight, compare, expression, news, insight, scene, weekly), and an admin can pick another theme when drafting or rewriting. Material is Daily's own lessons (the post date's lesson, or the latest weekday's on weekends, alternating 초·중급/고급; plus the week's expressions) and the newest article's vocabulary. The brand prompt lists the only Daily facts the model may claim (price per day comes from `DAILY_PRICE_PER_DAY_KRW`), forbids testimonials, invented numbers, URLs and Markdown, and asks every post to end with the free-first-day CTA. `insight` is the benefit-led format: a pain-point hook ("영어 회의에서 \"I think...\"만 외치는 당신에게"), the gap, before→after lines from this week's Daily expressions, what Daily gives, then the CTA.
- Cards (`server/cards.tsx`): text is laid out as unbreakable word tokens (`textTokens`) so Korean words and short quoted phrases never split across lines; next/og renders 1080×1350 slides in the site style with Noto Sans KR fetched from Google Fonts; sharp re-encodes to JPEG (Instagram accepts only JPEG); files go to `assets/social/{postId}/` with content-hashed names.
- Posting time (`server/slots.ts`): four KST slots per channel scored by the median of `growth_posts.metrics_24h.impressions` (views 24 h after posting, filled by the insights refresh). Explore until every slot has 4 scored posts, then post at the best slot and try the runner-up one day in five. Manual times are kept as `slot_reason = 'manual'`.
- Server modules use the service-role client; `/api/admin/social` checks the caller is an admin before any read or write. Never call these modules from client components.
- Reels (`server/reel.ts`, `server/voice.ts`): Instagram posts are carousels except Tuesday (expression) and Thursday (insight), which are reels; an admin can pick either format when drafting or rewriting. A reel draft is a carousel plus one narration line per slide. Each slide renders as a 1080×1920 frame (card inside Instagram's safe area, narration shown as a subtitle), Gemini TTS (same model and voice as One Cup Daily audio) reads each line, and ffmpeg from `@ffmpeg-installer/ffmpeg` (kept unbundled and traced into `/api/admin/social` and `/api/cron/social-drafts` via `next.config.mjs`) encodes an H.264/AAC MP4 at 30 fps; the first frame is the cover. Editing slides or narration rebuilds the video. The Edge Function publishes reels as `media_type=REELS` with `share_to_feed`, waiting up to ~150 s for Instagram's transcoding; a post stuck in `publishing` for 15 minutes is marked failed.
