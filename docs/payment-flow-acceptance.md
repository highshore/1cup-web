# Acceptance criteria

- A fresh checkout does not show a price before both region and product are selected.
- Region is always the first choice.
- Product selection is revealed only after region selection.
- Changing region clears the product selection and hides price/details again.
- The final screen uses the legacy compact payment visual style.
- The final screen shows scenario-specific full payment/refund text before the CTA.
- Existing subscribed users cannot buy another 30-day membership but can buy a 5-use pack.
- Regional catalog prices and checkout APIs remain server-authoritative.
