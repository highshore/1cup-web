# Checkout disclaimer coverage

## 30-day membership
- 30-day recurring billing and renewal notice.
- New purchase is scoped to the selected region.
- Referral is first successful paid purchase only, no self-referral, and a discounted membership keeps the actual discounted renewal amount while continuous.
- Full refund within seven days; after that, unused time is refunded pro rata from the actual paid amount.
- Existing active subscribers keep their grandfathered recurring amount; a new price applies only after ending and later rejoining.
- Profile link for subscription management and refund-policy link.

## 5-use participation pack
- One-time payment only; no membership start or renewal.
- Five uses, selected-region only, valid for 180 days; one use is consumed when meetup registration completes.
- Cancellation at least 24 hours before the meetup returns the used credit; within 24 hours it is not returned.
- Within validity, refund = actual paid amount × remaining uses ÷ 5. Discounted purchases use the discounted paid amount. Remaining credits are removed when refunded; expired packs are non-refundable.
- Referral is first successful paid purchase only and cannot be self-used.
- Profile and self-service participation-refund links plus refund-policy link.
